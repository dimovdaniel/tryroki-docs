<Update label="October 3, 2026">
  **New — Roki is now in the WordPress plugin directory.** Install [Roki – SEO Autopilot & AI Content Writer](https://wordpress.org/plugins/tryroki/) straight from **Plugins → Add New Plugin** in your WordPress admin — no zip upload needed — and get updates through WordPress. Version 1.1.0 syncs featured images, categories, tags, and SEO metadata for Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO, SEOPress, and Squirrly. See the [WordPress guide](/wordpress).

  **Fixed — Cleaner generated content.** Image sizes no longer get drawn as text onto generated images, and the "Pro Tip" label is no longer doubled in articles.
</Update>

<Update label="October 2, 2026">
  **Improved — A tighter subscribe page.** The trial and intro video boxes are more compact so plans show above the fold, the page is wider, and checkout is more reliable.
</Update>

<Update label="September 29, 2026">
  **New — Video embeds in the article editor.** Add YouTube videos or direct video URLs from new toolbar buttons. Embeds are full-width and responsive.

  **Improved — Faster onboarding from a domain link.** When you arrive with your domain already in the link, onboarding prefills your website for you.
</Update>

<Update label="September 23, 2026">
  **New — SEO report delivery settings.** Choose how and where your weekly SEO report is delivered from a new settings page.
</Update>

<Update label="September 21, 2026">
  **New — Redesigned weekly SEO drop.** Your weekly SEO tasks have a new look. The week view is split into a **Report** view and a **Tasks** view, with a verdict summary, a metric strip, eight-week trend charts, top movers, AI search (GEO) visibility, and a topic cluster map. Task cards are restyled with clearer details and time estimates, and last week's posts and backlinks now sit in the **Last week** tab.
</Update>

<Update label="September 12, 2026">
  **New — Redesigned pricing page.** The subscribe page has a clearer layout for comparing plans.
</Update>

<Update label="September 11, 2026">
  **New — Bring your own key.** Use your own AI provider API key for content generation and Roki's agents. A setup screen walks you through adding it.

  **New — One-time plans.** Plans can now be billed once instead of on a recurring period.

  **Improved — Lead magnets.** Free tools are now called **Lead magnets**, and their usage and backlink quotas follow your plan's limits.

  **Improved — Clearer empty SEO weeks.** A week without a report now says "No report for this week yet".
</Update>

<Update label="September 7, 2026">
  **New — Act on Content Gaps.** Open a keyword info modal, search the list, and add any gap straight to your content plan. A new **Get ideas** link sends a keyword to Idea Lab, and **Info** is now **Explore**.

  **Improved — Fewer competitors allowed.** You can now remove competitors even when you have fewer than three.
</Update>

<Update label="September 4, 2026">
  **New — Google Search Console for your AI agents.** A new Search Console MCP server lets your AI agents list your connected properties and pull organic search reports.

  **New — Filter and page through Content Gaps.** Filter gaps by competitor and choose how many rows to show per page. Competitor keywords now sync automatically when you add or remove a competitor.

  **Improved — Two-step Write article wizard.** The automatic **Write article** flow is now a simple two-step wizard. Planner cards show full titles, and duplicate draft posts are cleaned up automatically.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2, 2026">
  **New — Idea Lab.** Plan content around topics. Explore sub-topics, select them all at once, and add the titles you like to your content plan. Titles are kept SEO-friendly and use the current year.

  **Improved — Lead magnet previews.** Preview any free tool from a new **Preview** button.
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026">
  **New — Plan and schedule articles from the planner.** Build your content plan right in the planner and schedule articles through the **Write article** flow.

  **New — Multi-event webhooks.** Subscribe one webhook to several events at once, with a link to the [webhook docs](/webhooks/overview).

  **Improved — Reorganized menus.** The **Growth** and **Articles** side menus are restructured, and the Help Center opens in a new tab.
</Update>

<Update label="August 26, 2026">
  **New — Team members.** Invite teammates to your workspace. Invited people join automatically when they register, and owners can manage or remove members.

  **New — Search the articles archive.** Find any article fast with server-side search. The archive status column and planner dots now show each article's state.

  **Improved — Pause article creation from settings.** Stop or start article creation with a toggle in **Article settings**.

  **Fixed — Feature image regeneration.** Regenerating a feature image no longer fails for images served through our CDN.
</Update>

<Update label="August 25, 2026">
  **New — Intro video.** A short intro video now sits in the sidebar and on the subscribe page, with a quick link to the docs.
</Update>

<Update label="August 24, 2026">
  **New — 7-day free trial.** Start a 7-day free trial from **Choose Your Plan**.

  **Improved — Rename proposed titles.** Rename a proposed blog title from the planner idea modal.
</Update>

<Update label="August 21, 2026">
  **New — Image styles.** Pick image styles and brand colors for generated images. AI images are delivered through a fast CDN.

  **Improved — Planner cards show article titles.** Planner cards show the article title instead of the keyword, the instructions modal shows the title too, and the **Visit article** button is back.

  **Improved — Simpler onboarding.** Add your company website during onboarding to prefill content setup. The timezone picker is gone and defaults to UTC.

  **Fixed — Cover image styles.** Your chosen cover image style is no longer overridden by an older setting.
</Update>

<Update label="August 20, 2026">
  **Improved — Better feature images.** Feature image size is configurable, images no longer include stray captions or legends, and previews show the full feature image and title.
</Update>

<Update label="August 19, 2026">
  **New — Connect Roki to Claude and ChatGPT.** Sign in with OAuth from claude.ai, Claude Desktop, and ChatGPT, or use a connect token. ChatGPT gets search and fetch tools, and an install status page shows whether your connection works. See the [Claude](/mcp/claude) and [ChatGPT](/mcp/chatgpt) guides.

  **New — Article languages.** Articles show language flags, you can pick a language when writing an article, and you can clone an article into another language. You can also delete articles.

  **New — Write article from the planner.** Start an article manually from any content planner slot. Planner cards are redesigned, and the idea modal shows search volume and keyword difficulty.

  **Improved — Editor and content.** The editor has body counters and word count in article details, no longer shows a leave-site popup, and keeps the title, date, and feature image out of the article body. Markdown tables render correctly in content, previews, and webhooks. Source links are now nofollow, while backlink partners stay dofollow.
</Update>

<Update label="August 18, 2026">
  **New — Shareable article previews.** Share a polished, public preview of any article before it goes live, straight from a new **Preview** button in the editor. The preview page was refined to drop the duplicated title, date, and cover for a cleaner read.
</Update>

<Update label="August 17, 2026">
  **New — Reddit commenting opportunities on the content planner.** Spot Reddit engagement openings right alongside your scheduled content. Opportunities are deduplicated by URL and capped at five per day per cell, with a "+X more" indicator when there are extras.

  **New — AI SEO Report badge on the planner.** Planner cells now surface an AI SEO Report badge so you can see at a glance where a report is available.

  **Improved — SEO week reports release sooner.** Weekly SEO reports are now published as soon as their week begins, and the current week is included in your reports archive.
</Update>

<Update label="August 15, 2026">
  **New — MCP integration for your own AI agents.** Connect client-owned AI agents to Roki over the Model Context Protocol. Import and manage articles programmatically with the `upsert_article`, `list_articles`, and `get_article` tools, authenticated and scoped to your company API key. A new MCP integration page shows your endpoint and key, and MCP now appears in the Integrations grid.

  **Improved — Simpler onboarding.** Registration no longer asks for phone and address, site URLs are normalized automatically, and the color step is hidden to get you set up faster.

  **Fixed — Search result links stored correctly.** Fixed MariaHill search results not persisting their result link.
</Update>

<Update label="August 14, 2026">
  **New — A more capable article editor.** Edit article titles inline with a pencil action, track length with title and description character counters, remove sections with a new delete control, and keep things tidy with a Table of Contents that's collapsed by default.

  **Improved — State-aware editing and instant generation.** Edit affordances now adapt to an article's state, and content generation is triggered immediately instead of waiting.

  **Fixed — Cleaner images and on-brand prompts.** Fixed formatting around inline images, and stopped brand color palettes from leaking into image generation prompts.
</Update>

<Update label="August 13, 2026">
  **New — AI editing for article text and images.** Rewrite and refine your content and visuals with AI directly in the editor. Content Generation settings were regrouped into clearer sub-sections with a dedicated Branding tab.

  **New — Edit titles and slugs of unpublished articles.** Adjust the title and slug of an article before it's published.

  **Improved — Smarter onboarding and drafting.** Onboarding descriptions are now grounded in your scraped site metadata, the writing agent is aware of today's date, and today's draft is generated automatically once your content plan is ready.

  **Fixed — Webhook respects publish state.** The "Article made" webhook now fires according to an article's published/unpublished state.
</Update>

<Update label="August 12, 2026">
  **New — AgentaOS payments.** Added AgentaOS as a payment provider, with server-side checkout sessions for reliable attribution and automatic reconciliation of subscription cancellations and expiries.

  **New — More control from the planner.** Manually override the article type per slot, and trigger a "Generate now" run (impersonation-gated) directly from the planner.

  **Fixed — No more DataForSEO timeouts.** Keyword fetches no longer fail on the default 30-second timeout.
</Update>

<Update label="August 11, 2026">
  **Improved — See every article on busy days.** The planner now shows all articles scheduled on the same date instead of hiding the extras.
</Update>

<Update label="August 10, 2026">
  **New — Fury Browser Extension integration.** Connect the Fury Browser Extension with API-key authentication. It's listed and documented on the Integrations page.

  **New — Reddit commenting queue.** Added a commenting queue and a Reddit comments API to manage engagement opportunities in bulk.

  **Improved — Readable SEO task instructions.** SEO task instructions now render Markdown as formatted HTML, and the Fury workspace hides Posting Opportunities where they don't apply.
</Update>

<Update label="August 7, 2026">
  **Improved — Editable, better-styled SEO reports.** Member report sections and info boxes are now styled consistently and editable from the admin SEO tasks view.
</Update>
